# q02: Onslaught

What `q02.dll` does, as a spec to remake from. Read from Ghidra's decompile and disassembly (`../../decompile/q02/`).
The event system it runs on is in `mission-framework.md`; q02's copy is described below where its addresses or kinds
differ. The remake is `../../lua/q02/q02Lua.lua`, built on op2dll's draft; how it compares is at the end.

**Coordinates.** Tiles here are the DLL's internal ones: x is the map file's x + 32 on this 256-wide map (`Q02.map`),
and y is the file's. In status-bar coordinates that's (x − 31, y + 1). Key spots:

| Spot | DLL | Status bar |
|---|---|---|
| Evacuation point (static initializer `0x110015db`) | `(0x10D, 0x36)` | (238, 55) |
| Where Plymouth's reinforcements and convoys appear | `(0x21, 0x3C)` | (2, 61) |
| Volcano (the four set tiles, the eruption) | `(0xFD-0xFE, 9-0xA)` | (222-223, 10-11) |
| The single impassable tile | `(0xE2, 0x15)` | (195, 22), the fumarole |

**Players.** As in q01's spec: player 1 is the human (the DLL's player 0), player 2 Eden (DLL 1), and q02 adds
player 3, a Plymouth computer player (DLL 2) that is the human's ally and sends the pickup team.

**Difficulty.** 0 Easy, 1 Normal, 2 Hard, from player 1's difficulty (`0x1101C0F4`), with `0x1101C0F8` = difficulty × 2
and three flags `0x1101C0E8`-`0x1101C0EA` (is Easy / Normal / Hard). `InitMap` may overwrite all of them (below).

## The goal

You win by building up, researching, then evacuating the colony's core to the pickup point in the south-east. All
of these are victory conditions, so the game needs every one at once. There is **no failure condition**: q02 makes
none (no `CreateFailureCondition` import), and nothing in the DLL ends the mission in defeat.

**Made at the start** (`InitProc`, `0x110057C8`-`0x11005D96`). Each is an escape trigger for player 1 (repeating)
on the 12 × 12 rectangle at the evacuation point − 6, `(0x107, 0x30)`, status (232, 49) at its corner:

| Objective (text as in the DLL) | Unit | Cargo | Count: Easy / Normal / Hard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evacuate a Common Ore Smelter kit | ConVec | Smelter kit (`0x36`) | 1 |
| Evacuate a Structure Factory kit | ConVec | SF kit (`0x2F`) | 1 |
| Evacuate a Command Center kit | ConVec | CC kit (`0x1C`) | 1 |
| Evacuate a Tokamak kit | ConVec | Tokamak kit (`0x3A`) | 1 |
| Evacuate at least 1 Robo-Miner | RoboMiner | any (−1) | 1 |
| Evacuate at least 1 Robo-Surveyor | RoboSurveyor | any (−1) | 1 |
| Evacuate %d units of food | Cargo Truck | food (truck cargo 1), 1,000 each | 2 / 3 / 3 trucks (text: 2,000 / 3,000 / 3,000) |
| Evacuate %d units of Commom Metals | Cargo Truck | common metal (truck cargo 4), 1,000 each | 4 / 5 / 6 trucks (text: 4,000 / 5,000 / 6,000) |

The food and metal counts are `0x1101C100` and `0x1101C104`; the texts are formatted into `0x1101C168` and
`0x1101C1B8`.

**Added as you go** (the chain is in "What each event does"):

| Stage | Objective | Made by | Removed when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Easy only, from the start | Build a Standard Lab (operational Standard Lab count > 0) | `InitProc` `0x11005C60` | a Standard Lab works |
| After the lab (Easy), from the start (Normal), never (Hard) | Complete Research Training Programs (3305), Complete Offspring Enhancement (3304), Complete Cybernetic Teleoperation (3401) | `0x11001B31` | that topic's `Research…Done` |
| After the research (Easy, Normal), from the start (Hard) | Build a University / Build a Nursery / Build a Vehicle Factory (operational count > 0) | `Research3305Done` / `3304Done` / `3401Done` | that building works |
| After the Vehicle Factory | Have at least %d cargo trucks (food + metal count: 6 / 8 / 9); Have at least 4 convecs | `0x11002435` | never |
| After the pickup is called | Evacuate ALL your vehicles to (238,55) | `0x110021FA` | never |

- **"Removed".** The DLL destroys the satisfied victory condition and makes the next one, so the objectives list
  shows only what is left. University and Nursery only remove their own condition (`0x11002593`, `0x11002577`).
- **The research triggers** are game research triggers for player 1 that call the exported `Research3304Done`,
  `Research3305Done` and `Research3401Done` by name (`0x11001B5B`-`0x11001C12`).
- **"Evacuate ALL your vehicles".** Its trigger is a time trigger of 10 ticks, one-shot, made **disabled**
  (`0x1100239E`). A check every 100 ticks enables it when every vehicle of player 1 is inside the evacuation point
  ± 12, `(0x101, 0x2A)`-`(0x119, 0x42)` (`0x11004600`). With no vehicles at all, it is enabled at once.

## The start (InitProc `0x11005597`)

**Menu check.** The byte at `0x4E971C` must be `'Q'` (`0x11006BF2`). Otherwise a "Renegades" box says "Please run
Renegades missions from the Renegades game menu" and `InitProc` returns 0.

**Players** (`0x110055C9`-`0x11005712`):
- Player 1: Plymouth, human, colour 1, kids 0, tech level 2, allied with player 3. Research 2114, 3201 and 3408
  marked done.
- Eden: computer, colour 0, 10,000 food, 10,000 ore, tech level 12.
- Player 3: Plymouth, computer, colour 6, allied with player 1.

**Player 1's colony by difficulty** (set after the carry-over, `0x110059EA`-`0x11005BCC`):

| | Easy | Normal | Hard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common ore | 5,000 | 5,000 | 4,000 |
| Food | 3,000 | 3,000 | 2,000 |
| Workers | 30 | 26 | 24 |
| Scientists | 8 | 6 | 5 |

op2dll's draft has only one block, "Normal", holding Easy's numbers (30 workers, 8 scientists); Normal and Hard
differ as above.

**Order of the setup:**
1. Eden's two fixed bases from unit tables (`0x110012EE`, offset x + 0x1F): `0x11014BB0` and `0x11014BD0` (below).
2. Twelve beacons from `0x11014890` (`0x11001417`); they match the draft's `beacons`.
3. Lava-possible cells (`0x11004D2A`, table `0x11014C30`); they match the draft's `LAVA_POSSIBLE`. The same
   function sets the volcano's four tiles with `InitialSetTile`: `0x447` at `(0xFD, 9)`, `0x453` at `(0xFE, 9)`,
   `0x45E` at `(0xFD, 0xA)`, `0x469` at `(0xFE, 0xA)`.
4. Cell type 6 on the bottom row, x from the clipped maximum − 1 down to 0x1F (`0x11007330`), as in q01.
5. **`InitMap`** (below). If it fails, `InitProc` returns 0 and the game shows "Could not initialize game".
6. Eden's groups (`0x11004DAC`), Eden's patrols (`0x1100378F`), Eden's bases' building plans (`0x11002ECD`), the AI
   repair check (`0x11006CE5`).
7. Cell type 6 at `(0xE2, 0x15)` (`0x11005780`).
8. Morale free, daylight moves, not everywhere; the four cheats off.
9. The objectives, the colony by difficulty, the events (timetable below).

**Extra Eden Lynx by difficulty** (`0x110012EE` in the difficulty switch), all Laser Lynx for Eden:

|---|---|---|

## InitMap: the carry-over from q01 (`0x11004A36`)

Exported, but only `InitProc` calls it. It reads `Q01.mis` from the current folder (the game folder) with mode `rb`.
The file format is in "The carry-over q02 writes" below; the reader skips sections it doesn't need each time it
opens the file (`0x11006887`).

**What it rebuilds, in order:**
1. **Map cells** (`0x11006737`). For each record: dozed (`0x15`) and rubble (`0x16`) cells get their cell type and
   their saved tile; a normal wall (`0x17`) becomes a Wall; a tube cell `0x1A` becomes a Tube. Other recorded
   kinds (microbe and lava walls, the other tube cell types) are skipped. The cell-type names are the SDK's
   `CellTypes`. A read error shows a DEBUG box "Error reading file at %04X: (%04X,%04X)= %04X" (`0x11006829`).
2. **Player 1's structures**, then **Eden's structures** (`0x110064F3` with player 0, then 1). Every saved
   structure of that player is made at its saved tile. A Structure Factory gets its six bays back (kit and weapon).
   A Spaceport's six bays get the **second** saved dword of each pair as cargo, and a seventh bay the last pair's
   first dword when non-zero (as read, `0x110065D5`-`0x11006650`; probably a slip, q01 has no Spaceport).
   When the saved record is player 1's Command Center, the view centres on it, whether or not it is made.
3. **Vehicles** (`0x1100690B`) are read into a list; structures are dropped from it. **Eden's vehicles are never
   made**: only player 1's are, below.
4. **The difficulty check** (`0x11004A20`). The 0x20-byte block read from the file (`0x11016248`) starts with the
   difficulty q01 was played at. If it differs from player 1's, a "Renegades" box says "You're cheating !
   Difficulty reset to %s :-)" (easy / medium / hard), and the mission's difficulty variables take the file's
   value. The player's own difficulty setting is not changed.
5. **Moving player 1's vehicles** (`0x11004B5E`-`0x11004C93`), walking the list from its end:
   - for each vehicle, the closest unit to its saved tile that is a structure not owned by Eden;
   - for a Cargo Truck when player 1 has a Common Ore Smelter, the closest such structure that is a smelter;
   - a truck farther than 0x19 is put on that structure's tile;
   - any other vehicle (or a truck within 0x19) farther than 0x50 is put at the structure's tile − 5 +
     `GetRand(11)` in x, then the same in y.
   - The distance is the enumerator's, shifted right by 9; its unit isn't confirmed (see the report). The stand-in
     fits squared pixels shifted right by 9, i.e. 2 × tiles²: of q01's three trucks 3, 3.2 and 3.6 tiles from the
     smelter, only the last (2 × 13 = 26 > 0x19) was moved onto it (2026-11-19, from `runs/harness-1.3.6/q01.mis`
     and q02's start).
6. **Making them** (`0x110069DD`, `0x11006A19`), lights on:
   - Cargo Trucks are made **empty** (cargo and amount 0);
   - ConVecs keep their kit and weapon;
   - others are made with their saved weapon.
7. **The convoy's shortfall** (`0x1100491A`). Player 1's first Command Center is kept (`0x1101C01C`), its tile at
   `0x1101C0CC`. The rectangle CC − (6, 10) to CC + (10, 4) is kept at `0x1101C0D4`. Then, starting from these
   targets and subtracting each carried-over vehicle:

   | Kept at | Type | Target: Easy / Normal / Hard |
   |---|---|---|
   | `0x1101C108` | ConVec | 2 / 2 / 1 |
   | `0x1101C10C` | Cargo Truck | 4 / 4 / 3 |
   | `0x1101C110` | RoboMiner | 3 / 2 / 1, less player 1's Common Ore Mines |
   | `0x1101C114` | RoboSurveyor | 1 |

8. **Eden's Vehicle Factories** (`0x11004CAF`): one with x < 0xC9 is kept as the west factory (`0x1101C020`), one
   further east as the east factory (`0x1101C024`).

InitMap returns whether all three file reads succeeded.

## The timetable

**How the events behave** (q02's copy of the framework):
- **Makers.** Time `0x11007700` (active, flag2, min, max, cb, value), fixed time `0x11007770` (active, flag2, ticks,
  cb, value), unit count `0x11007840`, group loss `0x110075B0`, area `0x11007B70` (rectangle x, y, x + w, y + h).
- **Two more kinds** than q01:
  - operational-building count `0x11007420` (active, flag2, player, type, value, compare, cb, value): counts the
    player's structures of that type whose command is not Idle (`0x12`), Develop (`0x21`) or UnDevelop (`0x22`)
    (`0x11007210`);
  - two tile events. `0x11007A00` fires when any unit of the player stands on a tile (`0x11007910`).
    `0x11007104` fires when a structure of the player stands there with a command other than `0x1E`, `0x21` or
    `0x22` (`0x11007035`). Both put the unit found in the event's value for the callback. Command names are from
    the harness's `CommandName` table.
- **The second flag.** Set means one-shot, clear means repeating (`0x11007D10`: set gives bit 4, clear bit 2), as in
  q01.
- **Waits.** A time event's wait is drawn when it is made: the minimum plus `GetRand(max − min)`, or exactly the
  minimum when they're equal (`0x11007680`). A repeating one draws again after each run.
- **Order.** The draws come in the order below, which is the order `InitProc` makes the events. The event list runs
  from its newest event to its oldest each cycle.

## What each event does

### The objective chain

- **Research objectives** (`0x11001B31`). Removes "Build a Standard Lab" (`0x1101C134`) if it exists. Makes the
  three research victory conditions (`0x1101C13C` 3305, `0x1101C138` 3304, `0x1101C140` 3401). On Easy only, the
  message "New mission objectives" (`0x11001ADE`: the text at the player's first Standard Lab, sound `0x7A`).
- **`Research3401Done`** (`0x11001C7F`, once, flag `0x1101C118`). Removes the 3401 condition. Waits for an operational
  Vehicle Factory (`0x11002435`). Adds "Build a Vehicle Factory" (`0x1101C130`). Except on Hard, the message "New
  mission objective: build a Vehicle Factory". The east factory starts feeding the east guard (200).
- **`Research3304Done`** (`0x11001D41`, flag `0x1101C11A`), **`Research3305Done`** (`0x11001DEE`, flag `0x1101C119`).

## The carry-over q02 writes

On `DLL_PROCESS_DETACH`, only when the dword at `0x56EB34` is 3 (`0x11006BFF`, a won game: the harness ends runs as
won by writing 3 there), the DLL writes `<game folder>\q02.mis` (`0x11006C74`). The check is not inverted.

**Format** (the shared framework's writer, `0x11005FA7`):
1. **Cells** (`0x1100631D`, mode `wb`): a dword count of cells whose cell type is above `0xF` over the whole map.
   Then per cell: x (word), y (word), cell type (byte), and for dozed/rubble (`0x15`/`0x16`) the tile (word).
2. **Colony** (`0x11006491`, mode `ab`): eight dwords of player 1: difficulty, food, RLV count, kids, common ore,
   rare ore, scientists, workers.
3. **Units** (`0x11005FE6`, `ab`): a dword count, then for every player (highest first), structures then vehicles,
   0x18 bytes each: type, weapon, cargo, x, y, owner.
   - A truck stores its cargo amount as "weapon" and its truck cargo as "cargo".
   - A ConVec stores its kit and weapon, with the weapon zeroed when it carries no kit.
   - A Structure Factory or Spaceport is followed by six (kit, weapon) dword pairs.
4. Then it writes `music2.wav` in the game folder from the mission's `q02.mo3` (`0x11001106`, not traced).

**Two counting slips:**
- The writer's unit count adds running totals per player (`0x110060A3`), so it overstates the number of records
  whenever a player other than the last one written has units.
- Both readers read at most count − 1 records, stopping early at end of file (`0x11006542`, `0x1100694D`).
- So they read everything in practice. With only player 1's units in a file, though, the last vehicle would be lost.

## Other parts

- **`StatusProc`** (`0x11001ADA`) returns 0; **`NoResponseToTrigger`** (`0x11001ADD`) does nothing.
- **`AIProc`** (`0x11007CC0`) runs the event list at `0x1101C300` (`0x11007E90`); events come from the pool at
  `0x1101CB08`, map `0x11024B08` (`0x11007FD0`). `GetSaveRegions` is at `0x11007BE0`, the save stamp at `0x1101C2F8`.
- **DllMain** (`0x11005E63`): as the framework (renegmbl.raw at `0x1100107A`, music `q02.mo3`); on unload, after
  the carry-over, the music playlist is reset and the hook removed (`0x11005E31`).

## The remake against the original (2026-11-17)

**How it was compared.** As q01's:
- **The original:** `q02.dll` on OPU 1.3.6 (`C:\Users\Desktop\Tools\op2-136-check`), started from the harness's q01
  carry-over: `run-campaign.py <copy> OUT --difficulty D --ticks 30000 --play 2 --last 2`. q01 is ended as won at
  tick 2,900 as before, so its `Q01.mis` is byte for byte the one op2dll's draft was made from.
- **The remake:** `q02Lua.dll` on OPU 1.4.1 (`opu/op2testharness/game`).
- Both headless, nobody playing, for 30,000 ticks at each difficulty.

**What matches.**
- **Tick 1.** Every unit, of all three players, on the same tile with the same id; the same research for Eden.
- **The mission's events.** Plymouth's convoy and every reinforcement come on the same tick with the same units,
  at every difficulty. Easy's convoy brings a RoboMiner, a truck and four Lynx; Normal's a truck and two Lynx; Hard's
  nothing, as its start already has enough.
- No Lua errors in OP2Lua's log, and no crash.

**What differs: Eden's AI timing.**
- **When it starts.** From tick 100 to 500, small differences in what Eden's own AI does:
  - Easy: two east trucks a tile apart on their way to dock;
  - Easy: the west factory's first Lynx done about 100 ticks early;
  - Normal and Hard: the Earthworker starts its first tube line a few ticks early.
- **Why.** The mission's calls, the groups and their order, the target counts (2d + 2 is `0x11004DC5`) and Eden's
  research all match, so the cause is still open. The two runs are also on different game versions (1.3.6 and 1.4.1).
- **Where it ends.** By tick 30,000, Eden has 85 / 92 / 104 units in the original against 91 / 93 / 111 in the
  remake (Easy / Normal / Hard), with the repair AI's reach below.

**Found by the comparison and fixed.**
- **A carried-over truck's tile.** InitMap puts a truck that came from q01 far from the smelter onto the smelter's
  tile, and the game finds it a free spot: status (23, 25). op2dll's draft had it at (25, 22).
- **Lights.** The unit tables turn their units' lights on.
- **The repair AI's reach** (found with q03, the same code): only a ConVec within about 8 tiles is sent
  (`0x11006D88`, the distance's high word reaching 8).
- **An unchecked removal.** The south base (`0x11002C1C`) removes the closest ConVec, of any owner, from the west
  rebuild group without checking it's in it; the game faults on that (`../CRASH-LEADS.md` 11). The remake checks.

**Player 1's morale differs from the start** (0 against 45). That is the game versions, not the mission: the
original's colony shows 0 from tick 1 on OPU 1.3.6.

## Still open

- **Which building counts as "first".** Eden's Structure Factory and smelter "first" depend on the order the game
  lists buildings (`0x11002F41`, `0x1100262E`).
- **Distances.** The unit of `ClosestEnumerator`'s distances is unknown. They are shifted right by 9 before the
  0x19 and 0x50 tests (`0x11004BE3`), and the repair search stops at `0x80000` (`0x11006D88`).
- **Command names.** 0x12, 0x1C, 0x1E, 0x21 and 0x22 are named from the harness's command table, not the DLL.
- **Eden's AI timing in the remake** (above).
- **Not traced:**
  - the carry-over reader's Spaceport bays (`0x110065D5`);
  - whether enabling a trigger every 100 ticks restarts its 10-tick timer (`0x11004674`);
  - what `GetCargo` means for trucks at `0x110034B1`.
- **Unguarded repeats, not tested.** Once the convoy is past x 0xB8, the check at `0x110047A6` makes 6 more RPG Lynx
  and another event every 2000 ticks.
- **Reinforcements and the evacuation, inferred.** Reinforcements keep coming at (2, 61) while the player has fewer
  than 9 Lynx. That may spoil "all vehicles at the evacuation point".
- **No owner check.** `0x11002C1C` takes the nearest ConVec of any owner, so it could take the player's.
