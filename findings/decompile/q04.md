# q04: Raid

What `q04.dll` does, as a spec to remake from. Read from Ghidra's decompile and disassembly (`../../decompile/q04/`);
the functions that start with the EH prolog were read from the disassembly by hand. The event system it runs on is in
`mission-framework.md`. The remake is `../../lua/q04/q04Lua.lua`, built on op2dll's draft; how it compares is at the end.

**Coordinates.** Tiles here are the DLL's internal ones: x is the map file's x + 32 on this 64-wide map, and y is the
file's. In status-bar coordinates that's (x − 31, y + 1). The entry point, where the player starts and must bring
things back, is `(0x46, 0x78)`, status (39, 121).

**Players.** As in q01, numbered from 1: Player 1 is the human (the game's player 0), Player 2 the Plymouth base
(computer), Player 3 Eden (computer), Player 4 a passive Plymouth player that only ever owns the wrecked Panther.

**Map ids.** Past 0x42 the game's numbering is 0x46 ESG, 0x47 Stickyfoam, 0x48 Thor's Hammer (op2-missionhost
`lua/names.rs`). q04 uses 0x47 for Stickyfoam Lynx.

## Difficulty, and why it barely matters

q04 keeps the difficulty twice:
- a dword at `0x11017004`, set from Player 1's `Difficulty()` at the top of InitProc (`0x110044b3`);
- three flags, easy `0x11017000`, normal `0x11017001` and hard `0x11017002`.

**The flags are only ever set by the "You're cheating!" branch** (`0x110042e0`-`0x11004313`). Nothing else writes
them, and the `.save` section starts zeroed, so in a fresh, honest game all three are 0. Everything gated on the flags
then plays the same at every difficulty: never the Easy branch, never the Hard one. op2dll's draft found the same
("the setup makes the same at every difficulty").

**The check** (`0x11004252`, called from the carry-over loader). The 0x20-byte block read from `Q03.mis` into
`0x110118D0` holds Player 1's state at the end of q03, and its first dword is q03's difficulty. When it differs from
the current one:
- the message "You're cheating !\r\nDifficulty reset to %s :-þ", with easy/medium/hard, is shown in the game's message
  box, titled "Renegades";
- the dword and the three flags take **q03's** difficulty (`0x11017008` gets twice it, never read).

The player's real difficulty isn't changed. So only a player whose difficulty differs from q03's gets the flag-gated
content, at q03's level.

**What reads what:**

| Read from | What scales with it |
|---|---|
| Dword (the real difficulty, or q03's after the message) | Eden's assault size, the escorts' Lynx, Plymouth's late reinforcements |
| Hard flag | The extra start units (but see below), the second Vehicle Factory, the bigger attack group, a fourth hiding place for the parts and the Panther, the Panther plans in the second factory |
| Easy flag | Whether the attack group gets target counts at all |

The hard start units are tested at `0x1100459c`, before the carry-over loader can set the flag, so they are never made
at a fresh start, even after the message.

## The goal

The mission is the campaign's last. You win when every victory condition below holds at once. Each "Transmit" and
"Capture" objective is a special-target trigger: it's met when one of your units of the named kind reaches that
building. The texts are in `strings.tsv`; the triggers are made in `0x110034d5` unless noted.

| Objective (text) | Met when | Added |
|---|---|---|
| Capture Plymouth scientists | An Evacuation Transport reaches the lab holding the scientists | At the start |
| Transmit RPG data | A Scout reaches the Advanced Lab holding it | At the start |
| Transmit Rare Ore technologies | A Scout reaches the Rare Ore Smelter | At the start |
| Transmit Panther design plans | A Scout reaches the Vehicle Factory holding them | At the start (`0x110038db`) |
| Transmit security codes | A Scout reaches the building holding them | At the start |
| Spider prototype must survive | You have exactly 1 Spider | At the start (`0x11003cb1`) |
| Escort scientists to entry point | An Evacuation Transport with cargo 5 is in `0x40,0x72` + 12×12 | When the scientists are captured (`0x11003434`) |
| Destroy garages | Player 2 has no Garage | When the Panther plans are taken (`0x1100332c`) |
| Destroy vehicle factories | Player 2 has no Vehicle Factory | When the Panther plans are taken (`0x1100339d`) |
| Escort Panther to entry point | A Panther of yours is in `0x40,0x72` + 12×12 | When you take the Panther (`0x11003dfe`) |
| Bring unit parts to entry point | A Cargo Truck carrying cargo 9, amount 1, is in `0x40,0x72` + 12×12 | When the parts appear (`0x11003f1a`) |

- **The entry rectangle**, `0x40,0x72` to `0x4B,0x7D`, is status (33, 115)-(44, 126).
- **The Panther and the parts can be missed.** Their objectives are only added when the building hiding them is
  destroyed after its loss event exists (below). If that never happens, the win doesn't need them.

**You lose** (each failure condition has an empty text: it points at `0x11011788`, zero and never written):
- **no Evacuation Transport** (`0x11003767`);
- **no Spider** (`0x11003cfa`);
- **your last Scout dies** before the RPG data, Rare Ore tech, Panther plans and security codes are all taken
  (`0x11003d6a`);
- **a building holding something you haven't taken yet is destroyed** (table below);
- **after taking the Panther,** you have no Microwave Panther (`0x11003e44`);
- **after the parts appear,** you have no Cargo Truck at all (`0x11003f7d`);
- **after a truck loads the parts,** you have no truck carrying cargo 9 (`0x1100407b`).

The losses by building all go through `0x11003477`: a one-shot 10-tick time trigger tied to a failure condition, so
the mission ends 10 ticks later.

| Player 2 loses | You lose unless | Address |
|---|---|---|
| its Rare Ore Smelter | the Rare Ore tech is taken | `0x110041bf` |
| its Robot Command | the codes are taken, or they aren't in the Robot Command | `0x110041ce` |
| a Command Center (fewer than 2, then fewer than 1) | the codes are taken, or the building holding them is alive | `0x110041e6` |
| its Standard Lab | the scientists are taken, or `0x110170b4` is 1 | `0x11004204` |
| its University | the scientists are taken, or `0x110170b4` is 0 | `0x1100421c` |
| an Advanced Lab (fewer than 2, then fewer than 1) | the RPG data is taken, or the lab holding it is alive | `0x11004234` |
| its last Vehicle Factory | the Panther plans are taken | `0x110040a2` |
| (Hard flag) the first or second Vehicle Factory | the Panther plans are taken; only checked in the branch that makes the Panther | `0x110040de` |

- **The scientists' two checks are swapped.** `0x110170b4` = 1 puts the scientists in the Standard Lab, 0 in the
  University (`0x11003639`), yet each check fails you for losing the lab that does **not** hold them. Read from the
  disassembly; not measured.

## The start (InitProc `0x11004475`)

1. **Menu check.** The byte at `0x4E971C` must be `'Q'`, or "Please run Renegades missions from the Renegades game
   menu" and InitProc returns 0.
2. **Players.**
   - Player 1: Plymouth, human, colour 3, tech level 0, 10,000 common and 10,000 rare ore, allied with Player 4 (one
     way), research `0x3262` (12898) complete.
   - Player 2: Plymouth, computer, colour 1, tech level 12, 2,000 ore.
   - Player 3: Eden, computer, colour 0, tech level 12, research `0x3263` (12899) complete.
   - Player 4: Plymouth, colour 1; neither made human nor computer.
3. **Beacons** (`0x11010108`, 5 entries): mining beacons at `(0x48,0x5D)` and `(0x59,0x28)` (common) and `(0x2A,0x29)`
   (rare), and fumaroles at `(0x33,0x6D)` and `(0x40,0x59)`.
4. **Player 2's base** from the layout at `0x11010598`, then (hard flag, never at a fresh start) the one at
   `0x110105D8`, then **Player 1's** from `0x110105B8` (`0x110013d1`). Layout coordinates get +0x1F on x and −1 on y.
   Buildings and vehicles get their lights on. Tube lines are made along x first, then along y at the far end's x.
5. **The carry-over** (`0x11004267`, below). If it fails, InitProc returns 0 and the game says "Could not initialize
   game".
6. **View and light.** Player 1's view centres on `(0x46, 0x78)`. Daylight doesn't move and isn't everywhere.
7. **The hiding places**, random draws in this order (`0x110045fe`-`0x110046f3`):

   | Data | Draw | Picks |
   |---|---|---|
   | `0x110170a0` | `GetRand(3)`, `GetRand(4)` with the hard flag | where the unit parts are |
   | `0x110170a4` | the same | where the wrecked Panther is |
   | `0x110170a8` | `GetRand(2)` with the hard flag, else 0 | 1: the Panther plans are in the second Vehicle Factory |
   | `0x110170ac` | `GetRand(2)` | which Advanced Lab holds the RPG data |
   | `0x110170b4` | `GetRand(2)` | 1: Standard Lab, 0: University holds the scientists |
   | `0x110170b0` | `GetRand(3)` | which building holds the security codes |

   Then the parts' tile `0x11017094`, by `0x110170a0`. The y draw comes first.

   | `0x110170a0` | Building | x | y | Status |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | 0 | Vehicle Factory | `GetRand(3) + 0x3F` | `GetRand(3) + 0x2D` | (32-34, 46-48) |
   | 1 | first Garage | `GetRand(3) + 0x41` | `GetRand(2) + 0x23` | (34-36, 36-37) |
   | 2 | second Garage | `GetRand(3) + 0x41` | `GetRand(2) + 0x26` | (34-36, 39-40) |
   | 3 | second Vehicle Factory (hard flag) | `GetRand(3) + 0x38` | `GetRand(3) + 0x19` | (25-27, 26-28) |

   The tile there is saved (`0x1101709c`) to put back later.
8. **Setup functions, in order:** Player 2's groups (`0x11002b0d`), the objectives (`0x110034d5`), the mining
   (`0x11001d50`), the patrol (`0x11001f88`), then all of Player 1's units' lights off (`0x110031fd`).
9. **Events, in order:** the repair pass (`0x11005213`), the first Eden assault, Plymouth's reinforcements, the lights
   watch (below).

### The carry-over from `Q03.mis` (`0x11004267`)

Only the Lynx carry over. Everything else in the file is read and dropped.

**The file** (read from the game folder, mode `rb`; the same layout q04's own writer uses, `0x110048f0`, so q03's is
presumed to match):
1. map cells: a dword count, then per cell a short x, a short y and a byte cell type, plus a short tile when the type
   is 0x15 or 0x16. Skipped (`0x11004e65`).
2. a 0x20-byte block: difficulty, food stored, RLV count, kids, common ore, rare ore, scientists, workers (writer
   `0x11005017`). Read into `0x110118D0`.
3. units: a dword count, then 6-dword records (type, weapon, cargo, x, y, owner); a Structure Factory or Spaceport
   record is followed by 12 dwords of bays.

**Reading the units** (`0x11004ee9`). At most count − 1 records are read, stopping at end of file. Only vehicles are
kept (type below 0x11, or above 0x6E).

**The count** (`0x11004fbb`) counts Player 1's Lynx by weapon, but skips the first kept record. m = Microwave Lynx,
s = Stickyfoam Lynx.

**The split into 8** (`0x11004348`-`0x110043e7`):
1. t = (m + s) × 5 / 4. **If t is 0, the setup fails**: no Lynx at all in q03 means q04 can't start.
2. a = (10m + 10) / t Microwave, b = (10s + 10) / t Stickyfoam; spare = m − a.
3. While b > 4 and spare ≠ 0: one more Microwave, one fewer Stickyfoam, spare − 1.
4. While a + b < 8: a Microwave if spare > 0 (spare − 1), else a Stickyfoam.
5. While a + b > 8: one fewer Microwave if spare > 0 (spare + 1), else one fewer Stickyfoam.

**Making them.** a Microwave Lynx, then b Stickyfoam Lynx, for Player 1, facing North, on the slots of the table at
`0x11011800`, in order:

| Slot | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tile | `3F,7B` | `4D,7B` | `41,7B` | `4B,7B` | `3F,7D` | `4D,7D` | `41,7D` | `4B,7D` |
| Status | 32,124 | 46,124 | 34,124 | 44,124 | 32,126 | 46,126 | 34,126 | 44,126 |

**The overflow.** Steps 3 and 5 only stop on spare = 0, and spare can be negative, so with few Lynx a goes past 8 and
the slots run on into the next tables: slot 8 is the entry point `(0x46,0x78)`, slots 9-18 Eden's scout waypoints,
slots 19-25 the patrol route (slot 25 is `(-1,-1)`). Worked through the rule:

| m, s | a, b |
|---|---|
| 1, 0 or 0, 1 | 26, 0 |
| 2, 0 | 16, 0 |
| 4, 0 or 6, 0 | 10, 0 |
| 8, 0 | 9, 0 |
| 12, 0 or 16, 0 | 8, 0 |
| 4, 4 | 6, 2 |
| 10, 6 | 5, 3 |

op2dll's draft had 10 Microwave Lynx, the m = 4 or 6 case.

## What each event does

### The raid's loot

Each special target calls an exported callback. The decoys call `gotNothing`.

| Callback | Message (sound) | Also |
|---|---|---|
| `gotNothing` (`0x11003264`) | "Nothing found." (0x59) | |
| `gotRPG` (`0x11003279`) | "Retrieved RPG data." (0xB6) | flag `0x110178cc` |
| `gotRareOre` (`0x11003298`) | "Retrieved Rare Ore technologies" (0xB4) | flag `0x110178cd` |
| `gotCodes` (`0x11003458`) | "Retrieved security codes." (0xA0) | flag `0x110178cf` |
| `gotPanther` (`0x110032b7`) | "Retrieved Panther design plans" (0xB6) | flag `0x110178ce`; new objectives (below) |
| `gotScientists` (`0x110033c1`) | "Scientists captured" (0xB2) | flag `0x110178d0`; new objective (below) |

All messages go to Player 1 with no map position. "New objective" style messages use `0x11003250`, sound 0x7A.

**Where each thing is, and the decoys** (all Player 2's buildings, found with building enumerators; which one is
"first" is the game's enumeration order):

| Thing | Holder | Decoys (Scout or Evacuation Transport gets "Nothing found.") |
|---|---|---|
| Scientists (Evacuation Transport) | Standard Lab if `0x110170b4` = 1, else University | the other one |
| RPG data (Scout) | first Advanced Lab if `0x110170ac` = 1, else second; noted at `0x1101701c` | the other one |
| Rare Ore tech (Scout) | Rare Ore Smelter | none |
| Panther plans (Scout) | Vehicle Factory if `0x110170a8` = 0 | if 1: the Vehicle Factory is a decoy, and the objective waits on a 9,999,999-tick time trigger until the second factory exists |
| Security codes (Scout) | `0x110170b0` = 0: Robot Command; 1: first Command Center; 2: second Command Center; noted at `0x11017018` | the other two |

- The scientists' lab gets `SetCargo(5, 0)` when the target is made. Its purpose isn't known.
- The labs, Command Centers, Robot Command, Rare Ore Smelter, Vehicle Factory and the Garages all get
  `SetOppFiredUpon(0)`.

**`gotPanther`** adds:
- "Destroy garages" and "Destroy vehicle factories" (victory, counts == 0);
- garage loss events: Garages fewer than 2, then fewer than 1 → `0x1100415c`;
- the message "Destroy all vehicle factories and garages".

**`gotScientists`**:
- puts cargo 5 on the Evacuation Transport;
- adds "Escort scientists to entry point" (victory, an escape trigger: one Evacuation Transport with cargo 5,
  amount 0, in the entry rectangle);
- shows "New objective".

### The parts and the Panther

These appear when the building hiding them is lost. Each building-loss event checks both. The parts are made once
only (flag `0x110178d1`), and so is the Panther (`0x110178d2`).

| Event | Parts if `0x110170a0` = | Panther if `0x110170a4` = | Panther tile |
|---|---|---|---|
| No Vehicle Factory (`0x110040a2`) | 0 | 0, or 3 | `(0x40,0x2E)`, or `(0x3A,0x1A)` |
| (hard flag) first factory dead (`0x110040de`) | 0 | 0 | `(0x40,0x2E)` |
| (hard flag) second factory dead | 3 | 3 | `(0x3A,0x1A)` |
| First Garage dead (`0x1100415c`, only after `gotPanther`) | 1 | 1 | `(0x42,0x24)` |
| Second Garage dead | 2 | 2 | `(0x42,0x27)` |

Garages lost before the Panther plans are taken make nothing, so those parts or that Panther never appear.

**The unit parts** (`0x11003e72`):
- a wreck at the parts' tile (tech 0, visible), the tile set to `0x66F` and the cell type to 4;
- "Bring unit parts to entry point" (victory, an escape trigger: one Cargo Truck with cargo 9, amount 1, in the
  entry rectangle);
- a once-only event: Player 1 has exactly 1 Cargo Truck with cargo 9 → `0x11004027`;
- a failure if Player 1 has no Cargo Truck;
- "New objective".

**Parts loaded** (`0x11004027`): the saved tile is put back; you now lose if you have no truck with cargo 9. Whether
the game reports a truck's cargo as its "weapon" in the way this count expects is not checked.

**The wrecked Panther** (`0x11003fb2`):
- a Microwave Panther for **Player 4** at the tile, facing East;
- damage 250, `SetEMPticks(-1)` (EMPed for good), `SetOffensive(false)` (op2extra);
- a once-only event: Player 1 has more than 0 Microwave Panthers → `0x11003d79`.

How the player takes it over isn't in the DLL; probably the Spider's reprogramming (inferred: Player 4 is a separate,
non-allied owner, and the Spider must survive).

**Panther taken** (`0x11003d79`):
- `SetOffensive(false)` on the unit slot `0x11017054`, which nothing ever fills. It was probably meant for the Panther
  and has no use as written; a remake should leave it out.
- "Escort Panther to entry point" (victory, an escape trigger: one Panther, any weapon, in the entry rectangle);
- a failure if Player 1 has no Microwave Panther;
- "New objective".

### Player 2's base

- **Dozing** (`0x11001ffe`). Player 2's Robo-Dozer (`0x11017048`) clears the rectangles of `0x11011790` one after
  another. Every 400 ticks (repeating, `0x11002080`), when it isn't dozing (command ≠ 1), it starts on the next one.
  The event deletes itself after the last.

- **Repair pass** (`0x110054e9`, every 500). For each computer player, and each building type `0x15`-`0x3A` except
  Guard Posts:
  - a live building with 101 or more damage, not being dismantled, gets a repairer;
  - a ConVec already repairing it is left alone;
  - otherwise the nearest live, idle ConVec of that player with no kit is sent, within a distance limit (the upper
    word of the enumerator's distance below 8). A ConVec parked at a Structure Factory's dock is skipped. Failing
    that, a Repair Vehicle;
  - the repairer is noted per building (`0x11011934`, indexed by unit id × 7 + player).
- **Attack on lights** (`0x11003241`). The attack group stops patrolling and attacks. The player's units all start
  with lights off; the first vehicle turning them on sets this off.
- **Second factory** (`0x1100311d`, hard flag only).
  - Notes the unit at `(0x39,0x1A)` (`0x1101703c`).
  - The attack group moves from the first factory's reinforce list to the second's.
  - Makes the second factory a special target for Scouts: with `0x110170a8` = 1 it replaces the placeholder Panther
    objective (`0x110178e8`) with a real "Transmit Panther design plans"; otherwise it's a decoy.
- **Plymouth reinforcements** (`0x1100284d`, once, 18000-20000).
  - 2 × difficulty + 4 rows: on each, a Microwave Lynx at `(0x21, y)` and an RPG Lynx at `(0x20, y)`, facing East, y
    from 2 × difficulty + 0x15 down to 0x12. That's 8, 12 or 16 Lynx on the west edge.
  - They form the late guard: guarding `0x37,0x19`-`0x4C,0x29`, plus `0x1F,0x0C`-`0x5F,0x37` and
    `0x2A,0x37`-`0x54,0x62`, attack type 5 then 6, combined fire.
  - The rebuild follows 300 ticks later.
- **Rebuild** (`0x1100294d`, once). Counts Player 2's ConVecs (c), Structure Factories, Vehicle Factories, Tokamaks
  and Common Ore Mines, and whether a Command Center stands at x = 0x3D. Then, in this order:

  | If | Comes in from the west edge | To build at | Joins |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | fewer than 2 mines | Robo-Miner at `(0x21,0x10)`, East | | |
  | no mine | another at `(0x21,0x0F)` | | |
  | no Command Center at x = 0x3D | ConVec with a CC kit, `(0x20,0x0E)`; c + 1 | `(0x3D,0x1E)` | rebuilders |
  | no Structure Factory | ConVec with an SF kit, `(0x20,0x0F)`; c + 1 | `(0x42,0x1E)` | base builders if c was 0, else rebuilders |
  | no Tokamak | ConVec with a Tokamak kit, `(0x20,0x10)`; c + 1 | `(0x35,0x15)` | base builders if c < 2, else rebuilders |

  - A ConVec joining the rebuilders also has its building recorded there (`0x110027eb`).
  - The ConVecs are made with cargo = the kit.

### Eden's raid

- **Assault** (`0x1100244c`; first after 4200-4800, then again 2000 after each one is beaten, up to three).
  1. n = wave + 2 × difficulty + 2 (wave `0x110170c4`, from 0), minus 1 if Player 2 has fewer than 4 Guard Posts,
     minus 1 more if fewer than 2, minus 2 if it has no Vehicle Factory.
  2. n Railgun Lynx for Eden at `(0x55 + 2i, 1)`, facing South, join the assault group. The group attacks the enemy,
     preferring Guard Posts (attack type 0x17), with combined fire.
  3. The 2200-tick escort timer (`0x110178e4`), if any, is deleted. In 400 ticks the escorts come (`0x1100270a`).
     "Assault beaten" is cleared (`0x110178d5`).
  4. When a quarter of the assault group is lost → `0x11002783`. An empty assault (n ≤ 0) counts as beaten at once.
- **Assault beaten** (`0x11002783`).
  - The group guards its rect `0x52,0x0B`-`0x59,0x0E` again; the wave count goes up; "assault beaten" is set.
  - While the wave is below 3 and the four data aren't all taken: the next assault in 2000.
- **Escorts** (`0x1100270a`, 400 after an assault). Only while that assault isn't beaten.
  - Escort A runs while its waypoint index is below 10, escort B likewise.
  - If either ran, and this is the first time, a once-only event waits for Eden to have no Scouts; then, 2200 later,
    this check runs again.
- **Escort A** (`0x11002531`).
  - k = difficulty − (combat units in escort A, `UnitCount(0)`) + 1. When k ≥ 0, k + 1 Laser Lynx at
    `(0x5E − 2k + 2i, 1)`, South, join it.
  - If its Scout (`0x1101704c`) is dead, a new Scout at `(0x60, 1)`, South. That's one tile past the map's east edge
    (status x 65); where the game puts it isn't checked.
  - The group guards the Scout, follows it, rect `0x4A,0x16`-`0x4F,0x19`, attack type 5 then 0x17.
  - It resumes at its current waypoint.
- **Escort B** (`0x1100261f`). The same with its own group and Scout (`0x11017050`): k + 1 Laser Lynx at
  `(0x52 + 2k − 2i, 1)`, the Scout at `(0x50, 1)`.
- **Scout waypoints** (`0x11002244`, `0x1100235b`).
  - The waypoint index goes up by 2. Below 10:
    - the Scout moves to that waypoint;
    - its group's rect becomes the waypoint ± 2;
    - a repeating tile event waits for an Eden unit on the waypoint. When it's the Scout itself (`0x11002126`,
      `0x110021b5`), the event is deleted and the next leg starts 50 ticks later.
  - From 10: the Scout vanishes (`Poof`), its group leaves the map, and if the four data are all taken the assault
    group leaves too.
  - **Alliance.** When an index passes 3 and Eden isn't allied yet (`0x110020dd`):
    - "We might be able to help each other out!" (sound 0), then "It seems those Eden units are here for the same
      reason" (sound 0xB7);
    - Eden allies with Player 1 (one way);
    - flags `0x110178d4` and `0x110178d6` are set. `0x110178d6` is never read.
- **Waypoints** (`0x11011848`, static initializer `0x110019a4`). Scout A starts at index 0 (`0x110170bc` = 0), Scout
  B at 1 (`0x110170c0` = 1, set in the state constructor `0x11001cfb`).

### Ending

- `StatusProc` returns 0. Nothing else ends the mission: the game ends it on the victory or failure conditions.
- **On unload after a win** (end reason 3 at `0x56EB34`, `0x110047fa`): like the other missions, it writes `Q04.mis`
  into the game folder and renders `q04.mo3` to `music2.wav`. No mission reads `Q04.mis`.

## The remake against the original (2026-11-17)

**Update (2026-11-26, 1.2.20):** on OPU 1.3.6, both as campaign mission 4 (`../HARNESS-RUNS.md`), the remake still
differs from tick 5,000 on Easy and Normal (Eden's escort Scouts and Lynx a tile off, a Robo-Dozer's hit points), so
the drift below isn't explained by the event runner or the max tech level, both fixed in 1.2.20.

**How it was compared.** The original on OPU 1.3.6 from the harness's q03 carry-over: `run-campaign.py <copy> OUT
--difficulty D --last 4 --ticks 40000 --carry-ticks 5000` (q03 runs 5,000 ticks as before, so `Q03.mis` is byte for
byte the one op2dll's draft was made from). The remake on OPU 1.4.1. Both headless for 40,000 ticks.
- **Hard:** the original can't start from that carry-over (no Lynx, `../CRASH-LEADS.md` 10). The remake starts with
  eight Microwave Lynx on the slots (the decision of 2026-11-17) and runs to 40,000 with no errors.

| | Easy | Normal |
|---|---|---|
| Player 1's units identical | to the end | to the end |
| Player 2's units identical (type, tile, command) until | 5,100 | 5,400 |
| Eden's units identical until | 5,000 | 5,000 |

- **Up to 5,000 everything matches**: the start, the hiding-place draws, the dozing, Eden's first assault.
- **From 5,000** Eden's two escort Scouts each stand a tile off just after they're made; one starts off the map's east
  edge (`(0x60, 1)`). Why they differ is open; what follows drifts from there.
- No Lua errors, no crash, at every difficulty.

**Found by the comparison and fixed.**
- **A crash in the remake** (`../CRASH-LEADS.md` 12): player 2's Robo-Dozer died, the game gave its id to another unit,
  and the dozing step went on ordering that unit to doze. The remake now stops dozing with the dozer, and checks a
  kept unit's type before ordering it (q02 and q03 too).
- **The event cycle.** OP2Lua calls `on_tick` every 4 ticks, so the remakes' event runner works in 4-tick steps; the
  originals' events (q01-q03's comparisons) are consistent with that.
