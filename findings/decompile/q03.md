# q03: Consequences

What `q03.dll` does, as a spec to remake from. Read from Ghidra's decompile and disassembly (`../../decompile/q03/`).
The event system it runs on is in `mission-framework.md`. The remake is `../../lua/q03/q03Lua.lua`, built on
op2dll's draft; how it compares is at the end.

**Coordinates.** Tiles here are the DLL's internal ones: x is the map file's x + 32 on this 128-wide map, and y is
the file's. In status-bar coordinates that's (x − 31, y + 1). The player's carried-over vehicles are parked from
`(0x9D, 0x7C)`, status (126, 125). The unit tables the setup reads (`0x11014D40` etc.) already hold status-bar
numbers: the DLL adds `0x1F` to x and −1 to y when it uses them (`0x110059D8`).

**Players.** Player 1 is the human (game player 0, `0x11020128`), player 2 Eden (`0x11020138`), player 3 a computer
Plymouth (`0x11020118`); the three are copied from the game's `Player` array at load (`0x11001A63`).

**Difficulty.** 0 Easy, 1 Normal, 2 Hard, from player 1's difficulty (`0x110058E5`), kept at `0x1101C004`, with
flags for each at `0x1101C000`-`0x1101C002` and twice the difficulty at `0x1101C008`. `InitMap` can overwrite all
of these with the difficulty in `Q02.mis` (below).

## The goal

Victory conditions, so the game needs all of them at once.

| Objective (text) | Easy | Normal | Hard | Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| "Reconstruct the spider database" | yes | yes | yes | research 5052 (`0x11005A87`) |
| "Completely research Health Maintenance" | – | yes | yes | research 3303 (`0x11005AED`) |
| "Completely research Emergency Response Systems" | – | yes | yes | research 3301 (`0x11005B4D`) |
| "Completely research Mobile Weapons Platform" | yes | yes | yes | research 3851 (`0x11005BA5`) |
| "Research Vulcanology" | yes | yes | yes | research 3202 (`0x11005BE5`) |
| "Rebuild lost databases" | yes | yes | yes | set trigger: research 3401 and 3201, both (`0x11005C81`) |
| "Wait for Plymouth defectors", later "Escort defectors to safety" | yes | yes | yes | below |
| "Have enough evacuation transports for your population" | yes | yes | yes | evac trigger, player 1 (`0x11005D48`) |

- **Research triggers** are player 1's, made by `CreateResearchTrigger` (`0x110101A8`).
- **The defectors placeholder.** "Wait for Plymouth defectors" is a kit trigger for **player 2's** Spaceport kit (map
  id `0x37`), count 1 (`0x11005CC8`). Presumably it's never meant to complete: when the defectors arrive the DLL
  destroys this condition and makes "Escort defectors to safety" in its place (`0x1100268B`).
- **Objectives added by research.** Each research objective also has a one-shot research event of the DLL's own
  (`0x110080A0`, player 1), made at every difficulty. When the topic is done it adds a victory condition and shows
  "New mission objective: …" from player 1's first Standard Lab, sound `0x7A` (`0x11001F99`):

| Research done | Added objective | Trigger | Address |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5052 | "Have at least 1 Garage operational" ("Construct a Garage") | operational Garage > 0 | `0x11005748` |
| 3303 | "Have at least 1 Medical Center operational" | operational Medical Center > 0 | `0x11005670` |
| 3301 | "Have at least 1 DIRT operational" | operational DIRT > 0 | `0x110056DC` |
| 3851 | "Have at least 10 Lynx" ("Build 10 Lynx") | count of Lynx, any weapon, >= 10 | `0x11005823` |
| 3401 | "Have at least 2 Scouts" ("Build 2 Scouts") | count of Scouts >= 2 | `0x110057B4` |

  On Easy, researching 3303 or 3301 still adds the building objective, though the research itself isn't one there.

**You lose** when:
- player 1's colonists drop below 10, every difficulty (resource trigger, type 6, compare 3 `<`, `0x11005D01`);
- the defectors' Evacuation Transport is lost while being escorted (failure condition `0x1101C0E4`, made at
  `0x110026FA` with an empty text; its time trigger is enabled at `0x1100291A`, below).

## The start (InitProc `0x11005892`)

1. **Menu check.** Not started from the Renegades menu (`0x11006A26`): "Please run Renegades missions from the
   Renegades game menu", return 0.
2. **Players** (`0x110058DE`-`0x110059A7`):
   - player 1: Plymouth, human, colour 3, kids 0, tech level 2, tech 2106 (`0x83A`) done;
   - player 2: Eden, computer, colour 0, tech level 3, 10,000 common ore;
   - player 3: Plymouth, computer, colour 1, tech level 3.
3. **Beacons** (`0x110059C5`, table `0x11014458`, below). On Easy two variants change first (`0x110059B3`).
4. **Eden's two bases** from the tables `0x11014D40` and `0x11014D80` (`0x110059DB`, `0x110059EA`): buildings,
   tubes, walls, then units, all player 2, lights on. op2dll's `q03Lua.placement.lua` has them (its Eden list
   matches the tables).
5. **Lava** (`0x110049E2`), below.
6. **Map edge.** Cell type 6 (impassable) on the bottom row, from x = (clipped maximum − 1) down to 31 (`0x110074D0`).
7. **`InitMap`** (`0x1100487D`): the carry-over, below. If it fails, InitProc returns 0 here and the game says
   "Could not initialize game" (Eden's bases and the beacons are already made).
8. **Computer groups** (`0x11004A66`), Eden's mining (`0x11001FEC`), the first events (`0x11005629`), Eden's base
   plan (`0x110022F0`) and the repair AI (`0x11006B19`).
9. **Settings.** All four cheats off; daylight moves, not everywhere; initial light level `0x8F` (`0x11005A25`).
10. **Objectives** and research events (above).
11. **Stores** (`0x11005D79`): player 1 gets tech 3607 (`0xE17`), ore 0, and food; player 3 gets tech 12898 (`0x3262`).

| | Easy | Normal | Hard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Workers | 40 | 35 | 30 |
| Scientists | 15 | 12 | 10 |
| Kids | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Food (`[0x11010400 + 4d]` − 400, or − 200 on Hard) | 2,100 | 1,600 | 1,300 |
| Morale | `ForceMoraleGood` (`0x11005EF0`) | `FreeMoraleLevel` | `FreeMoraleLevel` |
| Volcano erupts at tick | 21,000 | 18,000 | 16,000 |

12. **Camera** on `(0x9C, 0x7C)`, status (125, 125) (`0x11005F29`).

**op2dll's draft.** Its three difficulties have identical colonists, food and research objectives, all Easy's, yet
Normal/Hard beacons. That fits the cheat reset below: its `Q02.mis` was written on Easy, so on Normal and Hard
`InitMap` reset the difficulty to Easy after the beacons were made. For the same reason the five Laser Guard Posts
and Standard Lab of player 2 in its placement list are Easy-only (`0x110023B6`).

### The carry-over (`InitMap`, `0x1100487D`)

**Reading `Q02.mis`** (from the current folder, `"rb"`):
- **The tile section** is skipped: a dword count, then records of x (2 bytes), y (2), cell type (1), plus a 2-byte
  tile when the cell type is `0x15` or `0x16` (`0x11006638`).
- **The header**, 0x20 bytes into `0x11016068`: difficulty, food, RLV count, kids, common ore, rare ore, scientists,
  workers (`0x110065F7`; the order is the writer's, `0x110068AB`). Only the difficulty is used.
- **The cheat check** (`0x11004868`). If the stored difficulty isn't player 1's, it shows "You're cheating !
  Difficulty reset to easy/medium/hard" (title "Renegades") and sets `0x1101C000`-`0x1101C008` from the stored one.
  If the file can't be opened the stored difficulty stays 0, so a Normal or Hard game without `Q02.mis` shows the
  cheat box, then fails.
- **The units** (`0x110066BC`): a dword count, then count − 1 records of six dwords {type, weapon or amount, cargo or
  kit, x, y, owner}, stopping at end of file. A Structure Factory or Spaceport record is followed by 12 dwords of bay
  contents, skipped. Only vehicles are kept (type < `0x11` or > `0x6E`, `0x11006898`).

**What carries over** (`0x1100472D`). Only player 1's vehicles (owner ≤ 0), each kind up to a limit, in file order;
the rest are dropped:

| Vehicle | Easy | Normal | Hard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cargo Truck carrying food (cargo 1), its load set to 200 | 2 | 2 | 1 |
| Cargo Truck carrying common metal (cargo 4), load kept | 4 | 3 | 2 |
| Cargo Truck, empty or anything else | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| ConVec with a Command Center kit / Structure Factory kit / Smelter kit / Tokamak kit | 1 of each | 1 of each | 1 of each |
| ConVec, empty or any other kit | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Lynx, any weapon | 4 | 2 | 0 |
| Robo-Surveyor, Robo-Dozer, Earthworker | 1 each | 1 each | 1 each |
| Robo-Miner | 2 | 2 | 1 |
| Anything else (Scout, Spider, Evac Transport, …) | all | all | all |

**Where they go** (`0x11004971`): the n-th kept vehicle (from 0) at x = `0x9D` − n/2, y = `0x7C` for even n and
`0x7D` for odd, facing East, lights on, with its weapon, truck load or kit (`0x1100678E`). So pairs fill westward from
status (126, 125).

## The timetable

**Event makers** in this DLL (all add to the list at `0x11020178`; the base constructor `0x11006EF0` sets flag 1 from
"active" and, from the second flag, 4 one-shot when set or 2 repeating when clear, so **set = one-shot**; a firing
event that isn't repeating goes inactive, and a one-shot one is deleted, `0x11006F30`):

| Maker | Arguments | Fires when |
|---|---|---|
| `0x110079C0` time | active, flag2, min, max, cb, value | tick reaches the due tick; wait = min + `GetRand(max − min)` at creation and after each repeat (`0x11007940`) |
| `0x11007A30` fixed time | active, flag2, ticks, cb, value | same, max = min |
| `0x11007B00` unit count | active, flag2, player, type, weapon (−1 any), value, mode, cb, value | count of the player's units (vehicles below type `0x11`, else buildings) compared with value |
| `0x11007870` working-building count | active, flag2, player, type, value, mode, cb, value | count of that player's buildings of the type whose current command isn't `0x12` (idle) or `0x21`-`0x22` (presumably develop/undevelop), compared |
| `0x11001121` group count | active, flag2, group, count, mode, cb, value | the group's unit count compared with count |
| `0x11007E50` group loss | active, flag2, group, mode, cb, value | as in q01: ≤ ¾ (mode 2), ≤ half (3), or none (other) of the count at creation |
| `0x11007CC0` area | active, flag2, player, x, y, w, h, cb, value | any unit of the player in `x,y`-`x+w,y+h` |
| `0x11001417` empty area | active, flag2, player, rect, cb, value | **no** unit of the player in the rectangle |
| `0x11008000` tile | active, flag2, player, x, y, cb | a unit of the player on that tile; the unit is passed as the value |
| `0x110080A0` research | active, flag2, tech, player, cb, value | the player has the tech |

Compare modes (`0x11007190`): 0 `==`, 1 `<=`, 2 `>=`, 3 `<`, 4 `>`.

**The dispatcher** (`0x110055FE`, every 100) runs:
1. Eden's attack manager (`0x11003E3C`) while `0x1101C0EA` is set (it starts set);
2. a check at `0x110042E0` while `0x1101C0E9` is set: nothing ever sets it, so it's dead code;
3. the transfer check (`0x11002867`) while `0x1101C0E8` is set (during the escort).

## What each event does

### Eden's base

- **Base plan, part 1** (`0x110022F0`, setup). The Builders record base table `0x11014D40` (buildings, tubes, walls,
  Guard Posts), a Standard Lab at `(0x30, 0xB)`, and a wall line `0x2D,0x26`-`0x21,0x24`.
  - **Easy:** the five Laser Guard Posts of `0x11014D60` and a Standard Lab at `(0x30, 0xB)` are made at once, then
    part 2 runs.
  - **Normal and Hard:** part 2 waits until Eden has a working Standard Lab.
- **Base plan, part 2** (`0x110021C8`). The Builders record a Nursery `0x31,0x12`, a University `0x2A,0xE`, a
  Vehicle Factory `0x32,0x1A` (its output goes to the Factory group `0x1101C06C`), Common Storage `0x2D,0xD`, the five
  Laser Guard Posts of `0x11014668`, and table `0x11014DC0`. Then, once Eden has a working Advanced Lab, 2200 ticks
  later (`0x11002292`) the Staging group wants 7 Laser and 4 Rail Gun Lynx and the Builders record three Rail Gun
  Guard Posts (`0x11014AB8`, `0x110022AB`).
- **Line recording** (`0x11001634`): as q01's. A straight line from end to end; a bent one along x, then along y from
  the corner, recording the corner twice.
- **Repair AI** (`0x11006DEF`, every 500, every computer player). For each building of types `0x15`-`0x3A` except
  Guard Posts (flag `0x110198CC`, 0):
  - damage below 101: its repairer (table `0x110160CC`, by unit id × 7 + player) is cleared;
  - otherwise, unless it's being dismantled or developed, and unless its repairer is a live ConVec still repairing
    (command `0x1C`): the nearest of the player's live, idle units within a short distance (stops when the
    distance's high word reaches 8, presumably 8 tiles) that's a Repair Vehicle, or a ConVec with no kit that isn't
    at a Structure Factory's dock (one tile up and one left of the ConVec for Eden, two right for other players,
    holds a Structure Factory, `0x11006B3B`), repairs it (`0x11006C6B`).

### Plymouth arrives (`0x11002CFE`)

At tick 12,000, and again later (below). Each time the arrival count `0x110200F4` (k) goes up by one.

1. **The column** at the west edge, player 3, into the Guard group: 4 + 2k Microwave Lynx at `(0x1F, 0x61)` facing
   NE, then 2 RPG Lynx at `(0x1F, 0x63)` facing N. The first time, 6 and 2. x = `0x1F` is one column left of the map.
2. **Clearing Eden's south base.** When no player 2 unit is left in `0x1F,0x47`-`0x2E,0x64` (empty-area event,
   repeating, `0x11020160`): Plymouth settles (`0x1100301D`).
3. **The first time only:** Mining 2 stops being reinforced, wants no trucks, and its units go to the Truck park.
4. **Reinforcements.** When the Guard group is down to half (`0x11020150`, `0x11002F76`): 1 + k Microwave Lynx and
   1 RPG Lynx at the same tiles, and the check is made again.
5. Eden's Factory now reinforces the Staging group at 8000.
6. One time in three (`GetRand(3)` = 0), 400 ticks later the South raiders take Eden's Laser Lynx in
   `0x20,0x3D`-`0x42,0x59` (`0x11003FA7`).
7. **From the second arrival on**, Eden's Builders group is rebuilt: a new group takes its units, the old one is
   destroyed, and the new one wants 2 ConVecs and 1 Earthworker, records table `0x11014D40`, part 2 of the base plan
   and the wall line again, and is reinforced at 400.

### Plymouth settles (`0x1100301D`)

Runs when Eden's south base is gone, and again 2000 ticks after Eden's attacks stop (below).

1. Removes the reinforcement and empty-area events; the Guard group's units go to Defence A, which guards
   `0x30,0x51`-`0x33,0x58` (guarding `0x33,0x4B`-`0x38,0x57` and `0x1F,0x43`-`0x33,0x53`).
2. Player 3's Builders group is rebuilt the same way (1 ConVec, 1 Earthworker, `0x26,0x4D`-`0x2A,0x53`) and takes
   player 3's ConVecs in `0x20,0`-`0x50,0x7F`.
3. **Mine.** If player 3's first Common Ore Mine has more than 800 damage it's destroyed. With no mine and no
   Robo-Miner, a Robo-Miner at `(0x1F, 0x5E)` builds one at `(0x27, 0x57)`.
4. **Smelter.** With none: the old smelter ConVec (`0x1101C038`), if alive, goes to the Exit group; a ConVec with a
   Smelter kit comes at `(0x20, 0x5C)`; the Builders record a Smelter at `(0x24, 0x50)`.
5. **Command Center.** With none: likewise (`0x1101C034`), a ConVec with a Command Center kit at `(0x20, 0x60)`, a CC
   recorded at `(0x24, 0x4D)`. A plain ConVec replaces `0x1101C034` if it's gone.
6. When player 3 has a Smelter: player 3's base, part 1 (`0x11003503`).
7. Eden's Miner builder wants a Robo-Miner and is reinforced at `0x7FFF`. While Eden has fewer than two mines or two
   smelters, it records a mine at `(0x65, 6)` and the Builders a Smelter `0x37,0xA`, Laser Guard Posts `0x65,3` and
   `0x65,9`, and a Tokamak `0x22,0x1A`. A repeating tile event on `(0x65, 6)` then sets up Mining 3 (`0x11002B0D`):
   the first units on `(0x65, 6)` and `(0x37, 0xA)` become its mine and smelter, rectangle `0x37,8`-`0x3B,0xC`,
   4 trucks, reinforced at 1200; with fewer than 3 in it (`UnitCount(7)`), the Truck park's units join.

### Player 3's base

### Eden's attacks (`0x11003E3C`, every 100 until stopped)

1. Compare the Staging group's count n with player 3's Guard Posts plus Lynx. **A wave goes** when n is larger, or
   when n is larger than the last wave's size (kept at `0x110200FC`, raised by one in that case; at least 5).
2. **Each wave:**
   - the first wave only: 900 ticks later, the South raiders take Laser Lynx (`0x11003FA7`);
   - all the Staging group's units join the Attack group;
   - if player 3 has a Common Ore Mine: when it has none (`0x110043C0`), Eden reinforces Staging at 8000 while player
     3 has Lynx, or else sends Staging into Attack and stops reinforcing it;
   - when player 3 has no Lynx (`0x11004240`): if it has no Guard Post either, Eden stops reinforcing Staging, sends
     it into Attack, its Builders record table `0x11014DA0` (Eden rebuilds the south base) and its salvage starts in
     500; and, unless one is pending (`0x11020158`), **Plymouth arrives again** in 3000-5000.
   - These one-shot events are added again on every wave, so several can be pending at once.
3. **Stopping.** After tick 50,000, or once the wave size passes 9 (checked on a wave): the manager stops, Plymouth
   settles again in 2000, and Staging is retargeted to 2 Scouts.
4. **Scout raids.** When Staging has exactly 2 units: in (3 − d) × 1000 to (3 − d) × 2000 (`0x11004564`), the raid
   (`0x1100440C`):
   - the Scouts go to the Scout patrol;
   - **below Hard, until player 1 has tech 3408 (`0xD50`):** again whenever Staging has 2 or more;
   - **otherwise** a counter (`0x11020170`) goes up, at most 5. Above 3, the patrol stops patrolling, prefers Guard
     Posts and moves to `0x6E,0x28`-`0x8C,0x50`. Staging then wants min(counter, 3) Laser Lynx, plus counter − 3 Rail
     Gun Lynx once player 1 has tech 3851 (without it the counter falls back to 3). Its units join the patrol, and the
     raid comes again when Staging has that many.
- **Salvage** (`0x110040E2`, then every 500 until it has a truck). With a live GORF, Eden's first empty, live Cargo
  Truck in Mining 1 that isn't on `(0x4F, 0x1A)` leaves the group and stops. The 200-tick event is switched on: while
  the truck and the GORF live, each time the truck hasn't moved since the last check and isn't carrying cargo 6, it
  salvages `0x20,0x40`-`0x36,0x60` for the GORF (`0x11003FE2`).

### The defectors

1. **An Evacuation Transport** (`0x1100247B`, at tick 80,000 or player 3's part 4). Player 3's Factory group reinforces
   the Evac group at 200. When player 3 has exactly one Evac Transport, step 2. If player 3 has no working Vehicle
   Factory (`0x11002425`), one Evac Transport (cargo 6) appears at `(0x22, 0x5A)` (status (3, 91)) instead.
2. **Loading** (`0x110024F3`). The Evac group's first unit of class 9 (presumably the transport) becomes the
   defectors' Evac (`0x1101C050`) with cargo 6. If player 3 then has no Evac Transport, back to step 1. 500 ticks
   later its group moves to `0x38,0x4B`-`0x3B,0x4E` (status (25, 76)-(28, 79)), and any player 3 unit entering that
   rectangle starts step 3 (`0x1100256D`).
3. **The defection** (`0x110025B8`, once: flag `0x1101C0EC`):
   - the Evac leaves player 3's groups and is given to player 1;
   - "A group of colonists wants to defect." from the Evac, sound `0xA2`;
   - a special target on the Evac for Residences (made disabled, `0x1101C0D8`);
   - "Wait for Plymouth defectors" is replaced by "Escort defectors to safety", met by a 10-tick time trigger made
     disabled (`0x1101C0DC`); a second disabled one (`0x1101C0E0`) drives the failure condition;
   - "New mission objective: Escort defectors to safety", then "To transfer colonists, put the evac next to a
     residence." (no sound);
   - the escort starts once no player 1 unit is left in `0x20,0x43`-`0x3E,0x5A`;
   - the escort-time checks run (flag `0x1101C0E8`).
4. **The escort** (`0x11002A51`). Up to four Microwave Lynx move from Defence A to the Escort group and are made
   **non-offensive** (op2extra `SetOffensive(0)`). Every 50 ticks (`0x11002929`) the Escort clears its guarded
   rectangles, `DoPatrolOnly`, and its rectangle becomes `x−2,y−2`-`x+3,y+3` around the Evac. The first time a player 1
   Lynx is within that rectangle grown by 4, the escorts are made offensive again, attack the enemy and prefer Lynx
   (flag `0x1101C0EB`); the 50-tick move order keeps being given after that.
5. **The transfer** (every 100, `0x11002867`). If the Evac is dead, the failure trigger is enabled: the mission is
   lost, presumably 10 ticks later. Otherwise, the first player 1 Residence in `x−1,y−1`-`x+2,y+2` around the Evac, if alive and
   not idled, while the Evac still has cargo 6 (`0x110027B8`):
   - "Colonists transferred safely." from the Residence, sound `0xB2`;
   - player 1 gets 5 scientists, 10 workers and 10 kids;
   - the Evac is emptied and its special target cleared; the special target and the failure condition are destroyed;
   - "Escort defectors to safety"'s time trigger is enabled, so it's met (presumably 10 ticks later).

## When the DLL unloads (`0x11005F7F`)

- **Loading:** the music `q03.mo3` from the mod folder, from order 1; the music hooks; `renegmbl.raw`.
- **Unloading:** if the game didn't end as won, it writes `q03.mis` into the game folder and renders the next music to
  `music2.wav` (`0x11006AA8`); either way it puts the graphics back, removes the music hook and sets the playlist.
- **The inverted check (`CRASH-LEADS.md` 7), confirmed.** `0x11006A33` returns whether the end reason `[0x56EB34]` is
  3 (won). q03 skips the write when it's true (`JNZ` at `0x11005FD4`); q01 skips when it's false (`JZ` at
  `0x110038B7`). So a won q03 writes no `q03.mis` and no `music2.wav`, and q04 can't start.
- **What `q03.mis` holds** (`0x110060C3`), the same layout `InitMap` reads:
  1. `"wb"`: the count of cells with cell type > `0xF`, then each as x, y (2 bytes each), cell type, and the tile for
     types `0x15`/`0x16` (`0x11006439`);
  2. `"ab"`: the 0x20-byte header for game player 0 (`0x110065AD`);
  3. `"ab"`: a unit count, then every player's buildings and vehicles, players from last to first. Factories and
     Spaceports add six (kit, weapon) pairs; trucks store (amount, cargo); ConVecs (weapon, kit) with the weapon zeroed
     when there's no kit (`0x11006102`).
  - The count adds running totals per player, so it's larger than the number of records whenever more than one player
    has units; readers stop at end of file. With only player 0's units it's exact, and the reader's count − 1 drops the
    last vehicle.

## The remake against the original (2026-11-17)

**Update (2026-11-26, 1.2.20):** on OPU 1.3.6, both as campaign mission 3 (`../HARNESS-RUNS.md`), the remake is the
same game as the original to the mission's end at every difficulty when nobody plays (lost at 10,504 / 9,992 /
9,480). Fed by script, it differs from tick 12,900 / 14,400 / 14,500: one of player 1's Laser Lynx attacks in the
remake and stays idle in the original; not traced yet. The drift below is from before the max tech level and event
runner fixes.

**How it was compared.** As q02's: the original on OPU 1.3.6 from the harness's q02 carry-over
(`run-campaign.py <copy> OUT --difficulty D --last 3`; its `Q02.mis` is byte for byte the one op2dll's draft was made
from), the remake on OPU 1.4.1, both headless.
- **Unfed, 30,000 ticks.** Nobody plays, so the colony starves and the mission is lost.
- **Fed, 90,000 ticks.** Both get `player 0 food 3000` every 1,000 ticks (`--script`), so the colony lasts and the
  later events run: Plymouth's arrivals, the volcano, Eden's waves and scout raids, the defectors.

| | Easy | Normal | Hard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unfed: the original ends (lost) | tick 10,504 | 9,992 | 9,480 |
| Unfed: the remake ends | 10,504 | 9,992 | 9,480 |
| Fed: the original ends | runs to 90,000 | 73,480 | 65,544 |
| Fed: the remake ends | runs to 90,000 | 73,480 | 65,544 |
| Fed: Eden's units identical (type, tile, command) until | 12,900 | 14,400 | 14,500 |

- **Unfed, everything matches to the end**: every unit of all three players, tile for tile, and the colony.
- **Fed, Eden's AI drifts after Plymouth arrives** (tick 12,000). On Easy one Lynx joins an attack wave at 12,900 in
  the remake but not in the original, and by tick 90,000 Eden has 2 Scouts against the original's 16. Why is open.
- No Lua errors in OP2Lua's log.

**Found by the comparison and fixed.**
- **Creation order.** The original makes all eleven beacons, then Eden's two bases. op2dll's placement made Eden's
  base before the two beacons that change with the difficulty, so every unit id was off by two.
- **Two of Eden's start tiles.** The Lynx and Rail Gun post of the second base stand at status (23, 38) and (26, 37) in
  the original; op2dll had (22, 38) and (25, 37).
- **Easy's morale.** `ForceMoraleGood` holds player 1 at 99, the top level, which OP2Lua calls "Excellent", not
  "Good" (75). With "Good" the colony starved 256 ticks later.
- **The repair AI's reach.** It only sends a ConVec within about 8 tiles: in the original a ConVec 50 tiles from the
  damaged building isn't sent (`0x11006BBC`, the high word of the distance reaching 8).
- **Crashes in the remake** (`../CRASH-LEADS.md` 11). The game's `ScGroup::TakeUnit` leaves a unit in its old group's
  list too, and removing it later from that older list, or twice, corrupts the list. The remake crashed so three
  times in fed runs. Now each unit is taken out of the group that last took it before another takes it.

**Player 1's research differs from the start** (12898 and 12899 on OPU 1.4.1 for every player, as q02's 5106). That is
the game versions, not the mission.
