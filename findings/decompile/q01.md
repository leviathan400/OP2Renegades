# q01: Relocation

What `q01.dll` does, as a spec to remake from. Read from Ghidra's decompile and disassembly (`../../decompile/q01/`).
The event system it runs on is in `mission-framework.md`. The Lua remake is `../../lua/q01/q01Lua.lua`.

**Coordinates.** Tiles here are the DLL's internal ones: x is the map file's x + 32 on this 128-wide map, and y is
the file's. In status-bar coordinates that's (x − 31, y + 1). The player's start, the Command Center, is
`(0x30, 0x1B)`, status (17, 28).

**Difficulty.** 0 Easy, 1 Normal, 2 Hard, from player 1's difficulty (`0x11012048`).

## The goal

You win by packing the base into kits and having the colony in shape to move. These are victory conditions, so the
game needs all of them at once.

| Objective | Easy | Normal | Hard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Have a Common Ore Smelter kit in storage | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Have a Structure Factory kit in storage | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Have a Command Center kit in storage | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Have a Tokamak kit in storage | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Store N units of food | 2,000 | 2,500 | 3,000 |
| Collect N units of Common Metals (the DLL spells it "Commom") | 4,000 | 5,000 | 6,000 |
| Have at least N total colonists surviving | 15 | 16 | 18 |
| Have at least N scientists surviving | 2 | 2 | 3 |

- **The kits.** Game kit triggers for player 1, one of each (`0x11002F9x`-`0x1100307C`).
- **The rest.** Resource triggers compared with `>=` (`0x110035D4`-`0x11003731`). The numbers are written into the
  texts with `scr_snprintf`.

**You lose** when any of these is true:
- colonists drop below 12, 16 or 18, by difficulty;
- scientists drop below 2, 2 or 3;
- you have no working Command Center (`0x11001461`).

On Normal and Hard the colonist goal and the colonist failure are the same number.

## The start (InitProc `0x11002D9F`)

The bases, the beacons, the colonists, the stores and the kits by difficulty are what op2dll's draft
(`q01Lua.placement.lua`, `DIFFICULTIES`) already makes.

**Map edits.** Cell type 6 (impassable) goes:
- on the map's bottom row, from x = (the clipped maximum − 1) down to 31 (`0x1100137f`);
- on one tile, `(0x96, 9)` (`0x11002EE1`).

**Settings.** Daylight moves, not everywhere; morale is free; the cheats are off.

**Eden's mine.** The setup ends by making Eden's Common Ore Mine at `(0x91, 0x1D)`.

## Eden's groups (`0x110029f1`)

All belong to player 2.

| West patrol (`0x11012024`) | same | Patrol `0x88,0x0E` → `0x57,0x17` → `0x30,9` → `0x28,0x30` → `0x2E,0x2C` → `0x8A,0x30`, lights on |
| Base guard (`0x11012028`) | `0x8B,3`-`0x9C,0x0A` | Guard the rectangle, lights on |
| Attack (`0x1101202c`) | `0x2D,0x15`-`0x36,0x1C`, beside the player's start | Attack the enemy, lights on |
| Tokamak guard (`0x11012030`) | `0x88,0x26`-`0x8E,0x2A` | Lights on |
| Raiders (`0x11012034`) | `0x2B,0x2C`-`0x2D,0x2E` | Lights off |

**Patrols.** The patrol routes are set with `SetPatrolMode` alone. Adding `DoPatrolOnly` after it, as the Lua first
did, sends the units to the map's corner instead (measured, below).

## The timetable

**How the events behave.**
- A time event's wait is drawn when it is made: the minimum plus `GetRand(max − min)`.
- A repeating one draws again after each run.
- The draws come in the order below, which is the order InitProc makes them.

| When (Easy / Normal / Hard) | What | Address |
|---|---|---|
| 4000-5000 / 3000-4000 / 2000-4000 | Scouts | `0x11001b5c` |
| 5000 / 5000 / 4000 | Eden's base builders | `0x11001f2a` |
| 7500 | Plymouth reinforcements | `0x1100246e` |
| 6000-8000 / 5000-7000 / 5000-7000 | First force | `0x110026c1` |
| 10000-13000 / 9000-11000 / 8000-10000 | Second force | `0x11002768` |
| 10000 | Plymouth reinforcements | `0x1100246e` |
| 4000-8000: once on Easy, repeating above | Meteor | `0x1100298c` |
| 2500-5500, repeating (Normal and Hard only) | Earthquake near the middle | `0x1100293c` |

**Area events.** Two of them (`0x110045c0`): either fires once, when any of player 1's units is in its rectangle.

| Rectangle | Fires |
|---|---|
| `0x87,1`-`0x9F,0x21` | The first force |
| `0x8A,1`-`0xA2,0x0B` | The second force |

**Count events.** All one-shot, polled every cycle:

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| Eden has no Common Ore Mine | The base builders |
| Player 1 has one Common Ore Mine | Extra Cargo Trucks after difficulty × 1000 + 2500 ticks |
| Player 1 has no Cargo Trucks | Extra Cargo Trucks after difficulty × 1000 + 500 ticks |

**Started last.**
- Eden's mining (`0x110022e9`).
- An earthquake anywhere, repeating 3000-7000 (`0x11002965`).
- The raid check, repeating every 3200 (`0x1100257a`).

## What each event does

- **Scouts** (`0x11001b5c`). Three Scouts appear at `(0x9E, 2)`, `(0x9E, 4)` and `(0x9E, 6)`, facing West, SW and SW.
  Each is in its own fight group with its own patrol route. The routes are filled in when the DLL loads
  (`0x110017b8`):
  - `0x78,0x0E` `0x60,6` `0x7D,0x24` `0x8E,0x29` `0x8E,0x0F`;
  - `0x88,0x2F` `0x5B,0x37` `0x54,0x17` `0x8D,0x0E`;
  - `0x8A,0x18` `0x5A,0x3A` `0x22,0x3C` `0x31,0x1A` `0x32,8` `0x55,0x17` `0x85,0x0A`.
- **First force** (`0x110026c1`, once only):
  - difficulty + 2 Laser Lynx at `(0x9E, 2)` join the north patrol;
  - above Easy, difficulty + 1 more at `(0x9E, 5)` join the base guard;
  - then the Tokamak guard is topped up (below).
- **Tokamak guard top-up** (`0x110024f0`): one Laser Lynx at `(0x9E, 3)`, two above Easy.
- **Second force** (`0x11002768`, once only):
  - two Laser Lynx at `(0x9E, 2)` and `(0x9E, 4)` join the west patrol;
  - above Easy, two more at `(0x9E, 4)` and `(0x9E, 6)` join the attack group.
  - When the attack group has none left, the wave is beaten. On Easy it's empty, so that fires at once.
- **Wave beaten** (`0x110028d5`):
  1. the next wave is due in 2000-4000;
  2. the reinforcement count (`0x11012050`, starting at 2) drops by one, and while it's above 0 Plymouth
     reinforcements come in 1000-2000;
  3. the next wave is one Lynx bigger, up to difficulty × 2 + 5. The first wave has 0 on Easy, 1 otherwise.
- **Next wave** (`0x11002841`):
  - that many Laser Lynx at `(0x9C, 4)`, each facing `GetRand(8)`, join the attack group;
  - when a quarter of it is lost, the wave is beaten again.
  - A flag at `0x1101205a` would also restart Eden's base builders, but nothing ever sets it.
- **Plymouth reinforcements** (`0x1100246e`):
  - three Microwave Lynx at `(0x21, 0x3C)` drive to `(0x2C, 0x1F)`;
  - the message is "Reinforcements have arrived", or "No more reinforcements available !" once the count is below 2.
  - Sound `0xA2`.
- **Extra Cargo Trucks** (`0x11001cfd`): three on Easy, two otherwise, from the same corner to the same tile, with
  "%d extra cargo trucks have arrived".
- **Eden's base builders** (`0x11001f2a`).
  - **The first time:**
    - a ConVec (West), an Earthworker (South) and a RoboMiner (West) at `(0x9E, 5)`;
    - a building group waiting at `0x94,6`-`0x9A,8`;
    - the group records:
      - the mine at `(0x91, 0x1D)`;
      - Laser Guard Posts at `0x89,6`, `0x8E,0x10`, `0x8F,0x19`, `0x8E,0x22`, `0x8A,0x10` and `0x93,6`;
      - Common Storage at `0x9B,5`;
      - a Tokamak at `0x8A,2`;
      - six tube lines (below);
    - the three vehicles and Eden's Structure Factory join the group;
    - every 1200 ticks, lost vehicles are replaced at `(0x9E, 5)` (`0x11001de7`).
  - **Later times:** only the replacement.
  - **Every time:**
    - Eden's Tokamak is noted;
    - a repeating 500-tick repair check is added (`0x11001e91`). It is added again on each call.
  - **The repair check.** When the Tokamak has 201 or more damage, a ConVec at `(0x9F, 0x3A)` (made with `0x40` as
    its cargo) repairs it. Once it's repaired, the repair group leaves the map.
- **Tube lines** (`0x1100163e`).
  - The six lines:
    - `0x8F,7`-`0x89,7`;
    - `0x8D,8`-`0x8D,0x10`;
    - `0x8C,0x10`-`0x8B,0x18`;
    - `0x8C,0x18`-`0x8F,0x18`;
    - `0x8E,0x19`-`0x8E,0x21`;
    - `0x8F,7`-`0x93,6`.
  - **How a line is recorded.** A straight line is recorded from one end to the other. A line whose ends differ in
    both x and y is recorded along x, then along y from the corner, so the corner is recorded twice.
- **Eden's mining** (`0x110022e9`).
  - **Until Eden has a mine and a smelter,** it tries again every 500.
  - **Then:**
    - a mining group runs between them, waiting at `0x8D,5`-`0x93,9`;
    - it takes all of Eden's Cargo Trucks;
    - every 500 ticks, when two or fewer are alive, one more comes at `(0x9E, 4)` (`0x11002254`);
    - the RoboMiner leaves the building group.
  - **When the mine is lost** (`0x11002412`):
    - a new RoboMiner comes (if the base builders have run);
    - mining starts again once there's one mine;
    - the base builders run.
- **Raid check** (`0x1100257a`, every 3200).
  - **Where the raiders wait.** With a player Tokamak, the raiders' guarded rectangles are cleared and they wait
    within 3 tiles of it. With none, they attack the enemy.
  - **Between ticks 9500 and 13500 of every 16000** (exclusive):
    1. Tokamak guards with y > `0x22` move into the raiders, until those number 4;
    2. on Hard, when a quarter of the raiders is lost, the guard is topped up again;
    3. the guard is topped up in 500-1000.
- **Disasters.**
  - **Meteor** (`0x1100298c`): at `(x + 0x1F, y − 1)`, size 1. x = `GetRand(0x6E)` and y = `GetRand(0x40)`, drawn
    again while (x, y) falls in `0x0D,0x15`-`0x19,0x1E`, the player's start.
  - **Earthquake near the middle** (`0x1100293c`): at `(GetRand(0x1E) + 0x40, GetRand(0x1E) + 8)`, magnitude 1. The
    y draw comes first.
  - **Earthquake anywhere** (`0x11002965`): at `(GetRand(100) + 0x20, GetRand(0x3E) + 1)`. The y draw comes first.

## The remake against the original (2026-11-15)

**How it was compared.**
- The original: `q01.dll` on OPU 1.3.6 (`C:\Users\Desktop\Tools\op2-136-check`).
- The remake: `q01Lua.dll` on OPU 1.4.1 (`opu/op2testharness/game`, with the newest OP2LuaCore from `op2luasdk/bin`).
- Both headless with OP2TestHarness, nobody playing, for 30,000 ticks at each difficulty. The telemetry was
  compared every 100 ticks.

| | Easy | Normal | Hard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Original ends (colony fails) | tick 25,096 | 17,164 | 11,016 |
| Remake ends | 25,096 | 17,272 | 11,016 |
| Eden's units identical (type, tile, command) until | 8,400 | 15,900 | to the end |

**What differs.**
- **Easy, from tick 8,500.** Eden's Earthworker picks a different first tube tile: `(141, 9)` in the original,
  `(141, 8)` in the remake. The six tube lines are checked against the disassembly, so the cause is still open.
- **Normal, from tick 16,000.** One Lynx is two tiles off in a fight.

**Found by the comparison and fixed.**
- **The patrols.** `DoPatrolOnly` after `SetPatrolMode` parked the scouts in the map corner.
- **The raid check.** It repeats every 3,200 ticks. It was first read as one-shot, with the event flags read
  backwards: the second flag set means one-shot, clear means repeating.

**Older OP2LuaCore builds.** The 0.7.0 core lacks `set_patrol` and `set_cell_type`, so the remake needs a current
one.
