OUTPOST 2: RENEGADES - mission files
====================================

Eddy-B's Renegades campaign (2004-2006), remade as OP2Lua missions for OPU 1.4.1 with OP2Lua 0.8.2.

  q01Lua ... q04Lua   Relocation, Onslaught, Consequences, Raid: each a .dll, its .lua script and .placement.lua
  Q01-Q04.MAP, .TEK   Renegades' own maps and tech trees
  ren228-234.wav      Renegades' voice lines, played by the missions

Play them through the loader, OPU\Renegades.exe: it starts each mission, shows Renegades' briefings
and keeps the campaign's progress.

Credits and install notes: OPU\renegades\README.txt.
